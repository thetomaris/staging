How we work

The stakes are high.
Our standards are higher.

i.

Candid discovery

One honest conversation with the supervising leader, the coachee and HR. Everyone agrees on what success looks like.

ii.

Your choice of coach

You interview at least two coaches, matched on leadership background, industry and style.

iii.

A plan you can measure

A customized strategic coaching plan with clear goals, so progress is visible, not assumed.

iv.

Quarterly reporting

Thorough reporting every quarter keeps sponsors informed and the return on investment in view.
