How we work

The stakes are high. Our standards are higher.

We understand the need for return and value on investment. Our success rates rest on practices that keep every party accountable from the first conversation to the final report.

Candid discovery

A direct conversation with the supervising leader, the coachee and HR, so requirements and desired outcomes are clear from day one.

Coach selection

You interview no fewer than two coaches, each matched on leadership background, industry context and coaching style.

Strategic coaching plan

A customized plan with defined goals, so progress is measured, not assumed.

Quarterly reporting

Thorough reporting every quarter keeps sponsors informed and the engagement focused on results.
