About Joe Patneaude

From the mailroom to the C-suite. Then off script.

Joe didn't start at the top. He started where the mail gets sorted.

Over more than 25 years in financial services, he worked his way through nearly every level of the business and into the C-suite. That path taught him something you can't learn from the corner office alone: how decisions look from every floor of the building, and how often a well-run process carries a company confidently in the wrong direction.

Out of that experience came STAR Scalability℠, his method for building growth that holds up, and his book, Follow the STAR: Unlock Monumental Business Growth.

Today Joe speaks to leadership audiences and advises a small number of executives privately. His message is simple and slightly inconvenient: before you trust the process, make sure it's pointed at the right problem.
