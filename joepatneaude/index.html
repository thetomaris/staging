01 The Keynote

Don't Trust the Process.

What to do when the solution isn't solving the problem.

Abstract

It starts with a personal health story. A case where every step was followed, every box was checked, and the outcome still didn't change. Because nobody stopped to ask whether they were treating the right thing.

Then the same pattern shows up in a business. Different setting, same mistake: a respected process, executed well, aimed at the wrong problem.

This talk is about the step most leaders skip. Diagnosis before prescription. It's not anti-process. It's anti-autopilot.

03 Point of view

Three things Joe won't pretend to believe.

01

"Best practice" is someone else's answer.

It solved their problem. Whether it solves yours is a separate question nobody likes to ask.

02

Diagnose before you prescribe.

Doctors learn this in year one. Leadership teams tend to learn it after the third failed initiative.

03

A checklist is not a strategy.

It's a very comfortable way to feel productive while the real problem waits patiently.
