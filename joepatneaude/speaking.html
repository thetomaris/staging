Don't Trust the Process.

01 The talk

Most fixes fail before they start.

Because they're aimed at the wrong problem. And nobody notices, because the process looks great.

Joe opens with a personal health story. Every step was followed. Every protocol was respected. The result still didn't change, because the treatment was for a problem that wasn't the actual problem.

From there, the talk moves into a real business case study. Different room, same pattern: a capable team, a respected process, solid execution, and an outcome that refuses to move.

The lesson isn't "throw out your process." It's that a process is only as good as the diagnosis in front of it. Joe shows audiences how to slow down at the one point where slowing down pays off: before the prescription.

Dry, direct, occasionally at his own expense. No seven-step framework. No standing ovation choreography.

02 Takeaways

What your audience leaves with.

Not a list of tips. A different reflex for the moment a problem shows up.

  • 01
    The diagnosis habit.How to tell the symptom from the problem before resources get committed to the wrong one.
  • 02
    When to question the playbook.Signals that a trusted process has stopped serving the outcome it was built for.
  • 03
    Values before metrics.Why the most useful questions in a turnaround are rarely about the P&L first.
  • 04
    Permission to say "this isn't working."How leaders make it safe for their teams to say the obvious thing out loud.
