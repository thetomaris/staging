The honest part

The part I won't pretend is easy.

Stepping away from your business for six days is a leap. It may mean asking your team to run the show, rethinking a week of client meetings, or asking someone at home to carry what you usually carry.

That preparation is leadership work in itself. It shows you exactly where the business depends on you, and where it doesn't have to.

Then there's the room itself: women you may not know, without your usual role or reputation doing the introducing for you.

But there is freedom in that too.

Something happens when thoughtful women step outside their normal lives at the same time. We ask different questions. We hear ourselves differently. Someone else's story loosens something in our own.

Possibility gets bigger in company.

And here is the question I'd ask you to sit with: if you make the same choice today that you made last month, where will you be on this exact day next year?

Nobody stays a stranger for long.

04 · Your host

I built this retreat because I needed it first.

I stepped into the landscape industry at nineteen and have spent four decades building a business while raising children, caring for family, leading teams, and navigating all the reinvention a long entrepreneurial life requires. I still lead that company today.

I know the questions this retreat is built around from the inside: when to hire, when to grow, when to simplify, and how to lead people without carrying all of them.

For a long time, I believed my ability to keep going was one of my greatest strengths.

It is a strength.

But I've also learned that endurance alone doesn't tell us where we should go.

Yoga, somatic practice, regenerative thinking, and years of personal inquiry changed the questions I began asking about my business and my life. I stopped focusing only on how to make the business work better and started asking what I actually wanted it to be working toward.

That inquiry changed how I lead.

I'm still doing the work.

I won't hand you a formula or tell you what your life should look like. Whatever your industry, I'll bring four decades of real business experience, deep curiosity, embodied practice, and a willingness to ask the questions that help you hear your own answers.

I'll build the container and invite the women.

Together, we'll see what becomes possible.
