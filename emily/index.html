Dear Emily,

It was so good to have you in community again on Tuesday. So I'm taking a bold step: I'm just going to invite you. I want you at ReRooted, and I want to tell you why.

You spent ten years doing deep, serious public work, and then you went out on your own so you could lead the work instead of supporting it. That takes nerve. I also know what the first years on your own look like: networking meetings, follow-ups, coffee dates, and a lot of creative energy spent looking for the next client instead of designing.

When I was writing my book, I was determined to write every single word myself. My coach asked me: is it more important that you write every word, or that the words get out into the world? I think a version of that question belongs to you. Is it more important that every client comes through your own door, or that your expertise and creative brilliance get out there, on real projects, in collaboration?

Landscape architects and garden designers are so often at odds, when each is strong exactly where the other struggles. You're someone who can work across that line. I'd love for you to bring that perspective into a room of women owners who need it, and to go home with a network of women who want to work with you.

Michelle, who is coming, really clicked with you. I think the rest of the circle will too.

I know you don't know me well yet. That's fair. So I'm not asking you to sign up from a web page. I'm asking for a conversation: thirty minutes, just the two of us, to talk about what you're building and whether ReRooted belongs in your next chapter. If it isn't right for you, I'll tell you.

Monique
Monique Allen · Founder & Host, ReRooted
