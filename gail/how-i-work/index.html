How I work

An ongoing engagement, built around your goals.

✦

The real progress happens in the work you do between our sessions.

  • Two sessions90 minutes each, every month, more when you need it
  • Nine monthsOne arc, from baseline to a plan you carry forward
  • RetainerInvoiced monthly, investment on request
The arc

Six movements.

Phase names: Gail to confirm

Months 1 to 2

Baseline

A 360, the goals that matter, and often a conversation with your sponsor.

Month 3

Commit

We agree how you want to do the work, and what you are willing to change.

Months 4 to 5

Stretch

The hard work. Your norm gets challenged and you show up differently.

Month 6

Notice

You see what has shifted. So do the people around you.

Months 7 to 8

Anchor

Stress pulls everyone back to old habits. We make the new way hold.

Month 9

Go Forward

Your plan to keep growing, without a coach.

i.

Leader effectiveness

One to one with you. Your presence, your decisions and the relationships that decide how far your impact reaches.

ii.

Team effectiveness

With you and your leadership team, so the people around you take ownership and carry the work as one team. Accredited as EMCC ITCA Practitioner for team coaching.

Focus areas Wording: Gail to confirm
i.

A bigger role

A new promotion, early traction, preparing for the next role.

ii.

Blind spots

Your style, your approach and how others experience you.

iii.

An accountable team

Results through others, ownership and judgement.

iv.

Vision and execution

The right priorities, sound decisions and alignment through conflict.

Fit

A small practice, by design.

I work with a limited number of executives at a time. When the practice is full, I keep a short waitlist, and new engagements start as soon as a place opens.

Fit is mutual. The clients who get the most from our work are committed, show up for every session and want to be challenged.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Who pays for the engagement?

Almost all engagements are paid by the company from its development budget and invoiced monthly.

What does my sponsor learn?

Only the goals, the progress toward them and your level of commitment. Everything else stays confidential, under the ICF Code of Ethics. Usually you report to your sponsor yourself, not me.

How much time does it take?

Two 90-minute sessions a month, plus the work you do between sessions.

How long does an engagement last?

Most engagements follow a nine-month arc, from setting a baseline to a plan you carry forward on your own.
