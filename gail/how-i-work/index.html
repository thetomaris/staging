How I work

Hard questions, from someone who has done the job.

✦

I don't tell you what to do. I ask the questions that challenge how you normally operate. The real progress happens in the work you do between our sessions, and that work belongs to you.

Presence

Before you lead the room, notice what happens in you.

Much of my work happens in the moment. I help you notice what happens in you before you speak: the urge to push, to fill the silence, to have the answer. Then you choose a different response, one that brings people with you.

Presence is a skill. It can be learned.

The engagement

Nine months, one direction.

i.Months 1 to 2

Baseline

A 360, the goals that matter, and often a conversation with your sponsor.

ii.Month 3

Commit

How you want to do the work, and what you are willing to change.

iii.Months 4 to 5

Stretch

Your norm gets challenged and you show up differently.

iv.Month 6

Notice

You see what has shifted, and so do the people around you.

v.Months 7 to 8

Anchor

Stress pulls everyone back to old habits. We make the new way hold.

vi.Month 9

Go forward

Your plan to keep growing, without a coach.

Fit

Who this is for.

CEOs, division presidents and senior executives in mid-sized companies, at a fast start in a new role or preparing for the next one. The engagement is almost always sponsored by the company.

A good signal: how quickly you say yes to a 360.

Not the right fitCases sent by HR or a boss to fix someone, startups, non-profits, first-time leaders, and anyone unwilling to look at their blind spots.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Who pays for the engagement?

Usually the company, from its development budget. The retainer is invoiced monthly.

What does my sponsor learn?

Only the goals, the progress toward them and your level of commitment. Everything else stays confidential, under the ICF Code of Ethics.

How much time does it take?

Two 90-minute sessions a month, plus the work you do between sessions.

How long does an engagement last?

Nine months, from a baseline to a plan you carry forward on your own.

What does it cost?

The investment is on request. We talk about it once we both know the engagement is a fit.

How do I start?

Put your name on the waitlist. I read every request myself, and if it looks like a fit, I invite you to a private conversation.
