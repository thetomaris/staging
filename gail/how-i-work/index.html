How I work

An ongoing engagement, built around your goals.

A monthly retainer with two 90-minute sessions a month, and more when you need it, within reason. The real progress happens in the work you do between sessions.

The arc

Nine months, six movements.

Every engagement follows the same arc, shaped around your goals. Phase names: Gail to confirm

iMonths 1 to 2

Baseline

A 360 assessment, clear goals and outcomes for our work, and often a conversation with your sponsor.

iiMonth 3

Commit

We decide together how you want to do the work, and what you are willing to change.

iiiMonths 4 to 5

Stretch

The hard work. We challenge your norm and you show up with new awareness.

ivMonth 6

Notice

You recognize what has already changed, and so do the people around you.

vMonths 7 to 8

Anchor

Under stress we fall back into old habits. We practice until the new way holds.

viMonth 9

Go Forward

You create the plan to keep growing without your coach.

Two disciplines

Leader and team effectiveness.

Most engagements start with the leader. Many grow to include the leadership team.

i.

Leader effectiveness

One to one with you. Your presence, your decisions and the relationships that decide how far your impact reaches.

ii.

Team effectiveness

With you and your leadership team, so the people around you take ownership and carry the work as one team. I hold the EMCC ITCA Practitioner accreditation for team coaching.

Fit

A small practice, by design.

I work with a limited number of executives at a time. When my practice is full, I keep a short waitlist, and new engagements start as soon as a place opens.

Fit is mutual. The clients who get the most from our work are committed, show up for every session and want to be challenged.

Investment on request.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who pays for the engagement?

Almost all engagements are paid by the company from its development budget and invoiced monthly.

What does my sponsor learn?

Only the goals, the progress toward them and your level of commitment. Everything else stays confidential, under the ICF Code of Ethics. Usually you report to your sponsor yourself, not me.

How much time does it take?

Two 90-minute sessions a month, plus the work you do between sessions.

How long does an engagement last?

Most engagements follow a nine-month arc, from setting a baseline to a plan you carry forward on your own.
