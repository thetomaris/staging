Case study · Utilities

From executive to division president.

✦

Industry
Utility business
Role
Newly appointed division president

He went from getting things done to leading the company.

i.

The goals.

He wanted to grow into a presidential mindset, and to build a network into the legislature and across his industry.

ii.

The turn.

The turn

“What I need.”“What matters to you?”

He used to call people about what he needed. Over our work he started asking what mattered to them instead. That one change built real relationships and goodwill for his region, on issues like rural internet access.

iii.

The result.

Two years later he pushed through a price increase that other divisions had failed to get approved.

Then he called me. “You just made the company $20M today.”

“One client stopped calling people about what he needed and started asking what mattered to them. Two years later, that was worth $20M to his company.”Gail Froelicher
