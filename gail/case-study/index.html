Case study · Utilities

From executive to division president.

$20M

Industry
Utilities
Role
Newly appointed division president

The goals

He wanted to grow into a presidential mindset, and to build a network into the legislature and across his industry.

The shift

He used to call people about what he needed. Over our work he started asking what mattered to them instead. That one change built real relationships and goodwill for his region, on issues like rural internet access.

From getting things done to leading the company.

The result

Two years later he pushed through a price increase that other divisions had failed to get approved. A year after our sessions ended, he called.

“You just made the company $20M today.”
