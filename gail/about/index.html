About

Gail A. Froelicher

✦

Founder, CEO and Executive Coach, Kinetic Insights, LLC. Columbus, Ohio.

Interim photo · shoot in December

Before I coached executives, I was one. I was the European Division President for a technology company, sat on the executive committee for four acquisitions and raised venture capital on Sand Hill Road. Today I work with CEOs and division presidents who want to be challenged by someone who has done the job.

✦

Turnarounds

Much of my corporate career was spent in turnarounds. Following a major restructuring, we exceeded 110% of revenue and 130% of EBITDA goals. We grew the customer base by 400% and the international reseller channel by 180%, and turned three underperforming sales regions into the top three divisions. I led and integrated a Deutsche Telekom division, took acquisitions from exploration through integration, and twice closed the largest deal in company history.

Where I started

I started in sales. I built reseller and referral programs, including partnerships with EDS and IBM, and was a 10-time President's Club awardee over 13 years.

Twenty years of coaching

For 20 years I have coached executives and their teams, more than 4,000 hours so far. I hold the ICF PCC, the ICF Mentor Coach Specialization (MCS), the EMCC ITCA Practitioner for team coaching and the Gestalt PCC. I chaired the coaching certification programs at the Gestalt Institute of Cleveland, leading 20 faculty, and built a Coaching Collaborative to support large coaching initiatives.

In a session

In a session I listen for the pattern underneath what is being said, and I ask the hard questions that challenge how a leader normally operates. The most impactful work happens with what emerges in the moment, because that is where new awareness is discovered. That awareness drives my client's work between sessions. Over the years I have learned that our work and our lives are always shaped by other people, and that relationship skills decide how big an impact a leader can have.

Boards

I have served on the boards of the National Association of Women Business Owners (Columbus), the Women's Council of Central Ohio, the ATHENAPowerLink Governing Body, the Ohio Department of Development Roundtable, and Make-A-Wish Ohio, Kentucky and Indiana.
