The Ninth Wave Method

The breakthrough rarely comes with the first wave.

It builds. Missed attempts are not proof that it can’t work. They are part of how it works. That is the idea behind the name, and behind the three phases.

1
Weeks 1 to 2 · The First Wave

Find the friction

Not willpower. We look for the small thing that derails the day: a folder that is never where it should be, sitting down at the wrong moment, one room that is bedroom, gaming room and study all at once.

2
Months 1 to 3

Remove it. Build rituals that fit.

Routines designed for how your student’s brain works, not for an average kid. First shifts usually show after three to four sessions.

3
Up to 12 months

Let it carry

New rituals turn into routine. Calmer days, steadier focus, better handling of big feelings. And over time, school tends to follow.

Is this right for you?

A good fit, and when it isn’t.

This is for families who

  • see potential in their student that isn’t showing up yet
  • have ADHD in the picture, diagnosed or not
  • want their student to feel understood, not managed
  • are ready for a weekly rhythm over a few months

This isn’t for you if

  • you are looking for a one-time fix
  • you need a diagnosis, therapy or medical care. Coaching works alongside those, it doesn’t replace them.
  • you need a guaranteed grade by a fixed date
Does my student need a diagnosis?

No. Diagnosed or not, we start with a conversation and see what fits.

Is this tutoring or therapy?

Neither. Coaching works on the habits, routines and feelings around school. It does not replace medical or clinical care.

In person or online?

In person in the Toronto area works best. Online is possible when in person isn’t.
