What we work on

Four areas where coaching helps

You might come with one of these on your mind, or with two or three. We start where it matters most to you.

Stakeholder management and communication

Manage up, across and down with confidence, and find the words that land.

  • Building trust with a new boss or leadership team
  • Getting peers aligned when priorities collide
  • Presenting to senior leaders with calm and clarity
  • Planning the conversation you've been putting off

Performance management

Set clear expectations, give feedback that helps and make the tough calls with care.

  • Agreeing on what good looks like, from day one
  • Giving feedback people can actually use
  • Handling underperformance fairly and kindly
  • Working out whether someone is in the right seat, on the right bus

Intentional career exploration

Make your next move an intentional one, then go get it.

  • Getting clear on what you want next
  • Interview prep and case studies
  • Negotiating your offer
  • Resigning gracefully, with your relationships intact

Values at work and in life

Get clear on what you stand for, and live it at work (and in life!).

  • Naming the values that matter most to you
  • Making decisions that match them
  • Seeing where work and life pull against them
  • Making changes you can keep

For organizations

Questions

Good questions, honest answers

How do I know if coaching is right for me?

If you're coachable and willing to be honest with yourself, you'll get a lot out of it. The clarity call is the easiest way to find out.

What happens on the clarity call?

We talk about where you are, where you want to be and what's in the way. If coaching makes sense, I'll recommend one clear next step. If it doesn't, I'll tell you that too.

What does coaching cost?

It depends on the format. I'll share exact pricing on our call, together with my recommendation, so you can decide with all the facts. Prices on the site?

Are sessions in person or virtual?

All sessions are virtual, so I work with clients all across the US. I'm based in Needham, Massachusetts, on Eastern Time.

What if something comes up between sessions?

Send me a message. Unlimited email and messages between sessions are part of every coaching package, and so is quick spot coaching when you need it.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Coaching with me happens in a safe, confidential and personalized space.

Can my employer pay for my coaching?

Some of my clients have their coaching paid for by their employer. If that's an option for you, mention it on our call and we'll take it from there.
