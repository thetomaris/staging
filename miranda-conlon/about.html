My story

From leading teams to coaching leaders

For more than 25 years I worked in B2B sales, operations and customer service at Wayfair, Staples and PricewaterhouseCoopers. I led global teams of up to 250 people and supported organizations of up to 3,000. 250 or 200?

I've worked in large, international and highly successful organizations, and I know what it's like to carry the numbers, the people and the pressure all at once.

Today I act as a trusted advisor to my clients. We work in a safe, confidential and personalized space, on the things that matter most to you right now.

How it started

A friend asked me a simple question: “Why aren't you a coach?” I started my business that same day.

How I coach

What you can count on

Direct and kind

“I'm very transparent, I'm very direct, but in a kind way.”

You'll always know where you stand with me.

Good humans

“I try really hard to only work with good humans.” "Good humans"

That means people who are coachable and willing to be a little vulnerable, at every stage of their career.

Right seat, right bus

“I believe strongly in ensuring that people are in the right seat and on the right bus.”

When people aren't, I help them and their leaders decide what to do next: coaching, development, a change in scope or performance management.

Winning or learning

“I'm either winning or learning, or both.”

Hard sessions count too. Each time, we look at what you can take into the week ahead.

Work and life, together

“I don't think you can talk about work without talking about life, and vice versa.” Quote OK?

Your life outside work belongs in the conversation too.
