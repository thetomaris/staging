How an engagement works

Clear, simple and built around your goals

  1. 01

    A clarity call about your team

    We talk about your goals, your people and what success looks like for you.

  2. 02

    Kickoff and 360 review

    Each leader sets their goals. Where it helps, a 360 review gives us an honest baseline.

  3. 03

    Six months of coaching

    One-on-one sessions with each leader, on the goals they set at kickoff.

  4. 04

    Check-ins with you

Questions

What sponsors usually ask

How long is a typical engagement?

Six months. That gives each leader enough time to practice new habits and make them stick.

Do you run 360 reviews?

Yes. A 360 review gives each leader an honest picture of how they show up, and gives us a strong starting point.

What do sponsors hear about the coaching?

Progress toward the goals we agreed on together. What a leader shares in a session stays confidential. Confirm wording

Is the coaching virtual?

Yes. All coaching is virtual, so I can work with leaders wherever they are in the US.
