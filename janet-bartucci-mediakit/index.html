About

Bio

Janet Bartucci is the founder of Conscious Living Coaching. She spent decades advising C-suite executives through pivotal transitions and brand evolutions at The Estée Lauder Companies and Hill & Knowlton.

Today she is a Certified Professional Core Energy Coach (CPC) and ELI-MP, helping leaders bridge the gap between success and fulfillment. Her signature framework, Gravitas, describes how you present yourself to clients and colleagues in a powerful, quiet way, and it is about everyday presence, not only how you act under pressure.

She coaches individual women in business and in career or lifestyle transitions, and works with teams and organizations through group coaching, ELI assessments and talks.
