    05 · How we will know it is working

    Three numbers. All of them free to collect.

    You said practical results and measurable improvements are what matter most. These get a baseline in month one and a review every quarter, in your own meeting.

    1. Decisions that get re-openedHow often the team revisits something it had already settled. The clearest signal that ownership is unclear.
    2. Escalations that reach youHow many cross-department handoffs need the CEO to step in before they move.
    3. The two divides from the diagnosticA short re-run on autonomy and on execution, so the gaps the reports found are tracked over time instead of assumed to be closing.
